## Full Description (≤ 4000 chars)

**Tanak Prabha — Scheme Information & Farmer Services**

Tanak Prabha is a mobile app that helps farmers and rural families find and understand
government schemes, connect with local professionals, and manage everyday farm records —
land, livestock, and crop details — all in one place.

> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:**
> Tanak Prabha is NOT a government app. It is an independent, non-government product
> created by a third-party developer. All government schemes, eligibility rules,
> benefits, and amounts shown in this app are gathered from multiple public sources
> for informational purposes only. They may change, may not be fully up to date, and
> are NOT official confirmation of your eligibility or entitlement. Always confirm
> scheme details on the official government website or at your nearest government
> office before applying.

**What you can do with Tanak Prabha:**

- **Browse Government Schemes** — Search scheme details, eligibility criteria, benefits,
  and application steps. Scheme data is informational and sourced from public
  government portals; the app is not affiliated with any government body.
- **Multilingual support** — Switch between Hindi and English to read scheme details in
  the language you understand best.
- **Farmer Services** — Connect with local professionals such as veterinarians and
  agriculture experts for guidance and appointments.
- **Farm Record Keeping** — Maintain your land details, crop seasons (Rabi, Kharif,
  Zaid), and livestock records securely on your device.
- **Local Information** — Get scheme and service information tailored to your district,
  block, and village where available.

**Who it's for:** Farmers, rural families, and anyone in India who wants a simple way to
stay informed about government schemes and manage their farm profile.

**Privacy:** Tanak Prabha values your privacy. Your personal data is stored securely and
is never sold. See the in-app privacy policy for details.

**Contact:** For questions or feedback, reach out to us through the contact details
listed in the app.

**Disclaimer:** Tanak Prabha is an independent third-party app and is not an official
government application, and is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by the
Government of India or any state government. The information provided in the app is for
informational purposes only and should not be treated as official. The app does not
replace official government communication. For official scheme information, please
refer to the official government websites and offices.
