# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Rural farmers and rural citizens in UP and similar states. Low digital literacy, primary language Hindi, using feature phones or budget Android devices. Users may have limited experience with apps and need clear, simple interfaces.

## Product Purpose

Enable rural citizens to:
- Access and apply for government agricultural schemes
- Connect with agricultural professionals (agronomists, veterinary experts)
- Attend and enroll in events and programs run by the NGO
- Access emergency/SOS contacts when needed

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, grounded, accessible. NOT modern startup vibes. The app should feel like a reliable government or NGO service, not a commercial consumer app.

## Anti-references

Should NOT look like Swiggy/Zomato/urban fintech apps. Avoid:
- Dark mode
- Micro-animations
- Glassmorphism effects
- Tiny tap targets
- English-heavy UI
- Sleek, polished consumer aesthetic

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over style** — Every element must be immediately understandable. If a user has to think, we've already lost them.
2. **Hindi-first** — All critical content available in Hindi. English labels only where necessary and always accompanied by Hindi.
3. **Big targets, big text** — Minimum 48dp touch targets. Base font size 16sp minimum. Budget devices need room for error.
4. **No surprises** — Standard navigation patterns. Users should never wonder "where do I go from here?"
5. **Works offline, works on cheap data** — Assume intermittent connectivity. Minimize heavy assets. Cache aggressively.
6. **Trust through transparency** — Show what's happening. Don't hide loading states. Confirm actions explicitly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as baseline
- Large tap targets (minimum 48x48dp)
- High contrast text (4.5:1 minimum ratio)
- Reduced motion support
- Hindi language priority; English as secondary
- Compatible with Android Go Edition devices
- Simple iconography with text labels (icons alone insufficient)
- Screen reader compatible
